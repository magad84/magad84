# SPH

**Procurement and Supplier Collaboration | Private product repository with public showcase**

SPH is an independent procurement and supplier-collaboration product designed to support a controlled buyer-supplier operating path across qualification, sourcing, and post-award execution.

The product is currently under development. Its implementation repository remains private while this public showcase presents the approved business direction without exposing internal workflows, protected logic, private data, or implementation details.

## Business Focus

- Supplier qualification and onboarding
- Governed sourcing workflows
- Buyer-supplier collaboration
- Post-award supplier execution
- Controlled procurement operating processes

## Product Principle

SPH is designed as an independent product. Future integration with other products may be considered only when explicitly approved and should not be assumed from its current architecture.
